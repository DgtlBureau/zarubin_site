The product

Everything a school runs on, in one place

Real screens from Revanta Academy — payments, scheduling, plans and communication.

Payments

Get paid on time, every month

Parents see exactly what's due and pay by card in two clicks — memberships, match entry fees and one-off charges, with the full payment history.

  • Online card payments with due, pending and overdue statuses
  • Competition sign-up with entry-fee payment and application status
  • Auto-generated payment links and debt reports with Excel export

Scheduling

One calendar for the whole club

Trainings, matches and tournaments across all groups and venues. Coaches create, move or cancel sessions — families see changes instantly and sync to their own calendars.

  • Club-wide calendar with hall and pitch allocation
  • Google and Apple calendar sync for parents and players
  • Attendance marking per session, visible to families

Subscriptions

Flexible plans for every group

Monthly memberships, trial sessions and one-off charges — set the price per group or per player, and let families pick and pay for a plan right in their account.

  • Membership tiers with active-subscriber counts
  • Registration form builder — enroll new students with payment
  • Parent invitations via link or code

Communication

Keep every family in the loop

Team chats, direct messages with the coach and a club news feed — plus notifications for charges, payment deadlines, schedule changes and match results.

  • Group chats per team and coach-parent direct messages
  • News and announcements for the club or selected groups
  • In-app notifications: billing, schedule changes, results

For every role

One platform, three workspaces

Parents, coaches and administrators each get their own view — connected by the same data.

Player & Parent

A personal account for everything around the child's training.

  • Pick a plan and pay dues online, full payment history
  • Attendance, calendar with Google/Apple sync
  • Match scores, standings and player statistics
  • Development plan, progress and video highlights
  • Chats with the coach, team chat and club news

Coach

The training process, from session plans to season stats.

  • Group calendar: create, move and cancel sessions
  • Attendance marking and group debt overview
  • Matches and tournaments: rosters, scores, match stats
  • Exercise library and individual development plans
  • Match video uploads linked to players

Administrator

Billing, enrollment and operations for the whole club.

  • Fee plans per group or player, payment gateway, pay links
  • Payment and debt reports with Excel export
  • Enrollment form builder and parent invitations
  • Club calendar with hall and pitch allocation
  • Club-wide news, notifications and a shared exercise library
