Who it is for
Professional clubsAcademies and youth schoolsFederations and leaguesArenas and event organisers

The club website is the first point of contact with a fan and the main source of organic traffic. The product covers the whole editorial job of a press office: a news item is assembled from a headline, summary, body and gallery, linked to a match and a category, given tags and a publication date — and goes live without a developer.

Matches, squads and statistics are not typed into body text: they live as separate entities in the system. A match has teams, score, arena, status, a link to the stream and to tickets; a player has a position, season statistics and a history of squad changes. The site assembles this data itself, so the score is updated in one place rather than in five news items.

A club with several teams and an academy does not need separate sites from separate contractors: content is tied to a club, permissions are split by section, and the fan stays the same profile as in the CRM, ticketing and loyalty.

What changes for each role

A news item goes out in minutes, not in approvals with a developer

  • Headline, summary, body and gallery — in one form.
  • A link to a match and a category instead of manual links in the text.
  • Tags and publication date, scheduled release and hiding from the site.
  • Video and photo galleries are managed in the same place as news.
