Revanta Loyalty — CRM and loyalty programme for a sports club

Discuss a rollout

Trusted by

Who it is for
Sports clubsLeaguesClub marketingCommercial team

The product turns scattered fan data into a database you can act on: profiles, purchase and attendance history, segments and communications.

The club grows engagement and revenue through the loyalty programme and personal offers, while marketing gets analytics and retention tools.

Capabilities

Loyalty programmePoints, tiers, personal offers and fan gamification.
One audience databaseFan profiles with a history of purchases, attendance and activity.
SegmentationFlexible segments for campaigns, promotions and targeted communication.
AI assistantPredictive churn analytics and prompts for growing revenue.
IntegrationsConnections to ticketing, the store, the app and payment services.
DashboardsEngagement, revenue and campaign performance metrics.
