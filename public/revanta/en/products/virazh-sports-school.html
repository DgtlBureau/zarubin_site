Revanta SportSchool — Digital platform for running a sports academy

What it solves
Academies3+ squadsIce hockeyCoaching staff

Schedules are assembled by hand in group chats, attendance and training load live in the coach's notebook, and fitness tests are recorded twice a year on paper — comparing a player with himself a year ago, or with his peers, is impossible, and squad decisions are made by eye.

Revanta SportSchool moves the whole training process into a system: from the coach's annual plan and venue load to a player card with test results and statistics. Management sees the academy as a whole, parents see their child's progress, and the methodology stays an asset of the school instead of leaving with the coach.

Capabilities

PlanningThe coach builds the annual plan and the monthly and weekly training cycles with hours allocated — the plan goes to the administrator for approval.
Scheduling and venuesThe administrator sees ice and hall usage across every team and coach, and places sessions without clashes.
Attendance and loadPresent, ill, absent — the type and volume of load, duration and the coach's notes. Parents get notified.
Players and testingThe player card: anthropometrics, statistics, coach ratings per match. A "Run testing" mode records results live.
Progress analyticsA player's season-by-season dynamics, a skills profile relative to the team, comparison between players — the basis for squad selection.
Reporting and controlThe coach's monthly report is produced in the system and goes to management for approval, with automatic reminders about unfilled plans.
